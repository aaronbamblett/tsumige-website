# Categories

Categories let you split your library into tabs (by genre, reading status, or however you like). A series can belong to more than one category.

## Manage your categories

Open the **Edit Category** screen from **Settings → Library → Categories** (also reachable from the **More** tab). There you can:

* **Add** a category (the + button) and name it.
* **Reorder** categories with the up/down arrows.
* **Rename** or **delete** a category. Deleting one moves its series back to the default category.
* Mark a category as the **default** for newly added series ("Default category when adding new manga to library").

The built-in default category can't be deleted.

## Hide a category

Each category has a **Hide category** toggle in the **Edit Category** screen. A hidden category stays in your library and keeps its series, but its **tab no longer shows** in the Library view. Use it to keep a private or NSFW shelf off the main screen. Turn the toggle back off to bring the tab back.

Pair this with [Incognito mode](/docs/faq/settings#does-tsumiru-have-an-incognito-mode) when you want to read without it touching your history.

## Add a series to categories

Open a series, then use its **Edit Category** option and tick every category you want it in (a series can be in several at once). Untick to remove it.

## Updating by category

When viewing a specific category tab in your Library, the overflow menu offers **Category Update** to refresh just that category, alongside **Global Update** for everything. You can also exclude entries from updates with the skip rules in **Settings → Library**. See the [Library FAQ](/docs/faq/library).
