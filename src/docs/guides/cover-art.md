# Cover art

1. Select a game in the library.
2. Open **More** in its details and click **Change art…**.
3. Choose **Cover** or **Background**. Covers, release package images and screenshots come from VNDB for games with a VNDB match.
4. Click an image, or click **From file…** to choose an image on your computer.
5. Click **Save** to apply your choices. **Cancel** closes the dialog without applying them.

You can choose both a cover and a background before saving.

## Reset an image

1. Open **Change art…** again.
2. For the original cover, choose **Cover**, then **Use VNDB cover**. This requires a VNDB match and a loaded cover.
3. To remove a separate background and use the cover instead, choose **Background**, then **Use the cover**.
4. Click **Save**.

If the online images fail to load, click **Try again**. Games added without a VNDB match can use **From file…**.
