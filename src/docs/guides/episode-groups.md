# Episode groups

Chapters or episodes of one series show as one tile with a count badge. The details pane has an episode picker under the title. A group opens on the episode you last picked, or on the first one you haven't played.

## Suggested groups

When your library holds titles that can be grouped, "N series can be grouped" shows above the grid.

1. Click **Review**.
2. For each series, click **Group** to group it or **Not a series** to dismiss it.

Tsumige suggests a group only when VNDB links the titles, through a shared entry or a prequel/sequel chain, and each title names a part: Episode, Ch., Vol., Part, 第N話 or 第N章. Sequels such as Steins;Gate and Steins;Gate 0 are not grouped.

## Group by hand

1. Ctrl+click or Shift+click several tiles to select them.
2. Click **Group…**.

To add a game to a group, open the group, Ctrl+click the game, then click **Group…**.

## Rename and ungroup

Select the group and open the menu (⋮) at the top of its details pane. **Rename group…** changes its name. **Ungroup** returns it to separate tiles.
